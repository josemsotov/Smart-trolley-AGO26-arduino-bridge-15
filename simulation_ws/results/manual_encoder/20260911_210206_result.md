# Manual left-wheel test

User instructed to turn left wheel 10 full turns, one direction; completion
confirmed with "done". Motors not commanded. Snapshot at completion (~101 s):

| Counter | Start | End | Delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Left opto | 703 | 1157 | 454 |
| Left Hall | 521 | 974 | 453 |
| Right opto | 638 | 638 | 0 |
| Right Hall | 616 | 616 | 0 |

Expected nominal: 450 pulses for 10 turns at 45 PPR. Left opto/Hall
discrepancy: 1 pulse, 0.22% relative to Hall. Offsets from nominal are
+4 opto and +3 Hall; manual rotation/end alignment uncertainty is not
measured, so do not change PPR or wheel diameter from this test.
Live endpoint verified with fresh telemetry and both motor PWM/RPM zero.
The recorder continues its fixed 180-second window; completion snapshot
above is the trial endpoint. This supports agreement in a manual slow test,
not reliability under powered motion or all speeds.
